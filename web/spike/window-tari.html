AgentPay — connect your own wallet (window.tari spike)

The same open/pay instruction mapping as flows A/B, but signed and submitted by the user's own wallet through the shared window.tari provider — the Sapient extension, or the Tari Universe web wallet. No backend, no user-run walletd, no browser-held key; every transaction is approved in the wallet's own popup.

Prerequisite not yet done: this reuses the spike's ootle.ts 0.11 / Ootle 0.47 instruction build, while the live testnet is post-reset 0.49.2 and current ootle.ts is 0.13 (→0.49.1). It will not round-trip on esmeralda until the spike deps are bumped (notably the 0.11.0 template-literal encoding change) and the indexer host is updated.
